Volumes, PersistentVolumes, and claims

Choose storage that matches the lifecycle and access needs of the application, from temporary scratch space to durable data.

1. Storage concepts

  • emptyDir: temporary space tied to a Pod's lifetime.
  • PersistentVolume (PV): a cluster storage resource.
  • PersistentVolumeClaim (PVC): a workload's request for storage capacity and access mode.
  • StorageClass: defines a class of storage and can enable dynamic provisioning.

2. PVC example

apiVersion: v1
kind: PersistentVolumeClaim
metadata:
  name: app-data
spec:
  accessModes:
    - ReadWriteOnce
  resources:
    requests:
      storage: 5Gi

3. Stateful workloads

StatefulSets provide stable Pod identities and can associate each replica with its own claim. They do not replace database backup, replication, restore testing, or application-level consistency planning.

4. Troubleshooting

kubectl get pvc,pv,storageclass
kubectl describe pvc app-data
kubectl describe pod <pod-name>

Look for Pending claims, unavailable StorageClasses, access-mode mismatches, zone constraints, mount errors, and filesystem permissions. Verify backups and restore procedures separately from the existence of a PVC.
